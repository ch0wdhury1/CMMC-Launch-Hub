# CMMC Launch Hub Sponsor Brief

Date: 2026-06-05  
Pilot Cohort: 3-5 initial organizations  
Target Market: Connecticut manufacturers and defense contractors

## Executive Summary

CMMC Launch Hub is a pilot-ready readiness operations platform for small and mid-sized defense industrial base organizations. It brings organization profile data, assessment workflow, evidence management, OCR, reporting, activity tracking, and operational monitoring into a single guided workspace.

The pilot is designed to validate value, usability, workflow completeness, and sponsor alignment for Connecticut manufacturers and defense contractors preparing for CMMC obligations.

## Sponsor Opportunity

Sponsors can help accelerate CMMC readiness for local manufacturers and suppliers by supporting guided access to a practical readiness workspace.

Sponsor-supported pilots may help:

- Reduce readiness confusion for small manufacturers.
- Encourage structured evidence collection.
- Improve visibility into common readiness gaps.
- Support a trusted local defense industrial base ecosystem.
- Generate anonymized learning about onboarding friction and support needs.

## Current Application Capabilities

- Organization and user management.
- Company Profile source-of-truth record.
- Organization Dashboard landing page.
- CMMC assessment navigation and save workflow.
- Evidence Library, evidence upload, OCR preview, view/download/archive.
- Executive Readiness Report.
- POA&M Report and POA&M workspace where tier allows.
- SPRS Scorecard.
- SSP PDF export where tier allows.
- Responsibility Matrix where tier allows.
- Activity Center for audit and support.
- SuperAdmin System Health dashboard.
- Sponsored, COMM_L1, and COMM_L2 tiering patterns.

## Pilot Design

| Area | Pilot Plan |
| ---- | ---- |
| Initial cohort | 3-5 organizations |
| Target participants | Connecticut manufacturers and defense contractors |
| Support model | Guided onboarding and direct founder support |
| Feedback model | Structured issue intake and weekly review |
| Success focus | Adoption, evidence workflow, report quality, role clarity, supportability |

## Sponsor-Facing Outcomes

The pilot should produce:

- Practical feedback from real manufacturers and contractors.
- Evidence of whether the dashboard and reporting reduce readiness friction.
- A support log of onboarding, evidence, OCR, report, and role-access issues.
- Success metrics for profile completion, assessment progress, evidence activity, report generation, and support response.
- Recommended improvements before broader launch.

## Important Boundaries

- CMMC Launch Hub is not a formal certification authority.
- The pilot does not replace qualified security, legal, or compliance advisory work.
- Billing, email notifications, and advanced remediation sequencing are not part of the current pilot.
- Sponsors should not receive organization-sensitive details without explicit participant permission.

## Recommended Sponsor Involvement

- Identify candidate organizations.
- Help communicate pilot purpose and expectations.
- Attend kickoff or summary sessions when appropriate.
- Review aggregate, non-sensitive pilot outcomes.
- Support next-phase expansion only after pilot readiness criteria are met.
