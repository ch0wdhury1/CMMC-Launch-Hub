# 1. Register

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Registration Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Commercial Registration

Use this option if your organization is joining directly.

### Steps

1. Open the registration page.
2. Enter company information.
3. Choose **Commercial Subscription**.
4. Select the requested CMMC level.
5. Enter your name, email, and password.
6. Submit.

### Expected Result

Your request is submitted for approval.

## Sponsored Registration

Use this option if your organization is joining through a state, sponsor, or program.

### Steps

1. Open the registration page.
2. Enter company information.
3. Choose **State / Sponsored Program**.
4. Select your program.
5. Select the available CMMC level.
6. Enter your name, email, and password.
7. Submit.

### Expected Result

Your request is submitted with program information attached.

> **WARNING**  
> If your sponsored program is not listed, stop and contact support or your sponsor before registering.

---

# 2. Wait for Approval

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Registration Submitted Page
]

------------------------------------------------

After registration, a platform administrator reviews your request.

## What Happens Next

1. Your registration remains pending.
2. An administrator reviews it.
3. If approved, your organization and account become active.
4. You can log in and begin setup.

> **TIP**  
> If approval is delayed, contact your sponsor or CMMC Launch Hub support.

# 3. Login

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Login Page
]

------------------------------------------------

### Steps

1. Open CMMC Launch Hub.
2. Enter your email.

# 4. Complete Company Profile

Estimated time: 8 minutes.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Company Profile Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Key Fields to Complete First

- Legal Name.
- DBA Name if applicable.
- Website.
- Primary Contact.
- Primary Contact Email.
- Primary Contact Phone.
- CAGE Code.
- UEI.
- NAICS Codes.
- FCI/CUI handling.
- Headquarters.
- Additional locations.
- External providers.

### Steps

1. Open **Company Profile**.
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Complete the key fields.
4. Save.

### Expected Result

Your organization profile becomes available for readiness reports and SSP generation.

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Do not skip CAGE, UEI, contact, and location fields if they apply to your organization.

# 5. Invite Team Members

Estimated time: 5 minutes.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Organization Invitations Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Recommended Initial Roles

| Person | Suggested role |
| --- | --- |
| Compliance lead | OrgAdmin |
| IT/security staff | Contributor or Assessor |
| Executive stakeholder | Viewer |
| External reviewer | Assessor or Viewer depending on access need |

### Steps

1. Open **Organization Invitations**.
2. Select **Add User**.
3. Enter name and email.
4. Choose role.
5. Send invitation.

### Expected Result

The invited user appears as pending until accepted.

> **TIP**  
> Start with only the users needed for the first readiness sprint. You can invite more later.

---

# 6. Start Your First Assessment

Estimated time: 5 minutes.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
CMMC Level 1 Assessment Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Steps

1. Open **CMMC Level 1** in the sidebar.
2. Select a domain such as Access Control.
3. Open the first practice.
4. Review the practice and objectives.
5. Choose an initial status.
6. Add a short note.
7. Save progress.

### Expected Result

Your dashboard and readiness summaries begin reflecting assessment progress.

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Complete the first five practices before trying to generate formal reports.

---

# 7. Upload Evidence

Estimated time: 3 minutes.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Evidence Upload Area
]

------------------------------------------------

## Steps

1. Open the relevant practice or **Evidence Library**.
2. Select the upload option if your role allows it.
3. Choose a relevant document.
4. Save or attach it to the practice.

### Expected Result

The evidence is available for readiness work and may be counted in summaries.

> **WARNING**  
> Upload only documents your organization is authorized to store in the platform.

# 9. Explore Marketplace

Estimated time: 5 minutes.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Marketplace Directory
]

------------------------------------------------

## Find Vendors

### Steps

1. Open **Marketplace**.
2. Search by company, category, role, state, or keyword.
3. Use filters such as category, remote availability, or featured vendors.
4. Open vendor details.

## Submit Reviews

If your role allows reviews:

1. Open a vendor profile.
2. Select **Submit Review**.
3. Enter rating and comment.
4. Submit for moderation.

## Track Vendor Engagement

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Working With This Vendor Modal
]

------------------------------------------------

### Steps

1. Open a vendor.
2. Select **Working With This Vendor**.
3. Set status such as evaluating or active.
4. Save.

### Expected Result

The vendor appears in your vendor engagement list.

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Mark vendors as evaluating before selection and active only after your organization has actually begun working with them.

## Quick FAQ

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the platform certify compliance? | No. It supports readiness work. |
| Can I use it before approval? | No, full access requires approval. |
| Why is Level 2 locked? | Your tier may not include Level 2. |
| Can viewers upload evidence? | No. Viewers are read-only. |
| Can sponsors see my evidence files? | No. Sponsors see sanitized summaries only. |
| What should I do first? | Complete Company Profile, then start assessment. |
