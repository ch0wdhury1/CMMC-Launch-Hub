# Introduction

CMMC Launch Hub helps organizations organize their CMMC readiness work in one secure platform. This workbook teaches you by doing. You will use the platform as a real user and complete tasks that mirror normal onboarding and readiness workflows.

This workbook is not an API guide, developer guide, or architecture manual. It is a lab-based training resource.

## What You Will Practice

- Registering as a commercial or sponsored organization.
- Logging in and using navigation.
- Completing Company Profile fields.
- Inviting team members.
- Completing an assessment practice.
- Uploading and reusing evidence.
- Generating reports.
- Searching Marketplace.
- Submitting marketplace reviews.
- Tracking vendor engagements.
- Applying readiness best practices.
- Completing scenario-based exercises.

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Use a training or QA organization for workbook labs. Do not use real customer production data unless the training session has been approved for production use.

# Module 1: Platform Overview

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Log in to CMMC Launch Hub.
- Identify the Command Dashboard.
- Locate Marketplace.
- Locate Compliance Reporting.
- Identify key navigation areas.

## Estimated Time

20 minutes

## Difficulty

Beginner

## Prerequisites

- Active user account.
- Approved organization or assigned observer account.
- Platform URL.

## LAB 1.1: Login

### Steps

1. Open CMMC Launch Hub.
2. Enter your assigned email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Login**.

### Expected Results

- Organization users land in the organization experience.
- Sponsor/Program Observers land in the sponsor observer experience.
- SuperAdmins see SuperAdmin navigation options.

3. Find evidence, report, and recent activity summaries.
