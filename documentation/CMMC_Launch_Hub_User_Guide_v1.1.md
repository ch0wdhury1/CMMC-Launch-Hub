# 1. Welcome

## About CMMC Launch Hub

CMMC Launch Hub is a secure software platform that helps organizations organize and manage their CMMC readiness work. It gives teams one place to review practices, track assessment progress, upload evidence, generate readiness reports, coordinate users, and engage with marketplace vendors.

The platform supports both commercial customers and organizations participating through sponsored programs.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Welcome or Login Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## What Problems It Solves

CMMC readiness work usually involves many people, files, decisions, and status updates. CMMC Launch Hub helps reduce that complexity by providing:

- A structured CMMC Level 1 and Level 2 assessment workspace.
- A Company Profile used by reporting and SSP generation.
- Evidence storage and reuse.
- OCR and evidence intelligence assistance.
- Readiness dashboards and domain progress summaries.
- Reports for executives, POA&M tracking, SPRS scoring, and SSP preparation.
- User roles for administrators, contributors, assessors, and viewers.
- Marketplace vendor discovery and engagement tracking.
- Sponsored program visibility for authorized sponsor observers.

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Treat CMMC Launch Hub as your operational readiness workspace. Keep your profile, assessment statuses, notes, and evidence current so reports remain useful.

## Commercial vs Sponsored Programs

CMMC Launch Hub supports two enrollment paths.

| Enrollment path | Who uses it | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial Subscription | Organizations joining directly as customers. | The organization requests CMMC Level 1 or Level 2 access. Subscription/payment activation may be handled after approval during pilot use. |
| State / Sponsored Program | Organizations joining through an approved sponsor or program. | The organization is assigned to a specific program, such as a state or sponsor pilot, and may be visible to authorized program observers in summary form. |

> **NOTE**  
> Sponsored program observers see only sanitized program oversight information. They do not see raw evidence files, private notes, AI conversations, or full report contents.

## User Roles

Your role controls what you can see and do.

| Role | Primary use |
| --- | --- |
| OrgAdmin / OrgOwner | Manage organization profile, users, assessment, evidence, and reports. |
| Contributor | Help complete assessment work and evidence tasks. |
| Assessor | Review readiness, evidence, and assessment progress. |
| Viewer | View readiness and reports without making changes. |
| Sponsor Observer | View read-only sponsored program oversight dashboards. |
| SuperAdmin | Platform administration role. Not normally used by customer teams. |

> **WARNING**  
> If a feature described in this guide does not appear in your account, your role or tier may not include access to that feature.

# 2. Getting Started

## Creating an Account

Accounts are created in one of two ways:

1. Your organization registers and waits for approval.
2. An OrgAdmin or SuperAdmin invites you.

After your account is active, you can log in using your assigned email and password.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Registration Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Commercial Registration

Use commercial registration when your organization is joining directly as a customer.

### Purpose

Commercial registration creates a pending organization request for platform review and approval.

### Who Uses It

Usually the first OrgAdmin for the organization.

### When to Use It

Use this flow if your organization is not joining through a sponsored program.

### Steps

1. Open the registration page.
2. Enter your company information.
3. Select **Commercial Subscription**.
4. Choose the requested CMMC level.
5. Enter your user information.
6. Create a password that meets the minimum password requirement.
7. Submit the registration.

### Expected Outcome

Your registration is submitted for review. You cannot access the full application until the request is approved.

> **NOTE**  
> During pilot operations, commercial payment activation may be handled outside the application after approval.

## Sponsored Registration

Use sponsored registration when your organization is joining through a state, sponsor, partner, or other approved program.

### Purpose

Sponsored registration links your organization request to a specific program.

### Who Uses It

The first user registering a sponsored program participant organization.

### When to Use It

Use this flow only if your organization has been instructed to join through a sponsored program.

### Steps

1. Open the registration page.
2. Enter company information.
3. Select **State / Sponsored Program**.
4. Select the sponsored program from the dropdown.
5. Select the available CMMC level.
6. Enter your user information.
7. Submit the registration.

### Expected Outcome

Your organization request is submitted with program information attached. After approval, your organization is assigned to that sponsored program.

> **TIP**  
> If the sponsored program you expect is not listed, contact your program administrator or CMMC Launch Hub support before submitting registration.

## Approval Process

Registrations do not automatically activate full access. A platform administrator reviews pending registrations and either approves or cancels them.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Registration has been submitted and is waiting for review. |
| Approved | The organization and initial user account have been activated. |
| Cancelled | The registration was not approved or was withdrawn. |

## Logging In

### Steps

1. Open CMMC Launch Hub.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Login**.

### Expected Outcome

If your account and organization are active, the application opens to your dashboard or assigned landing page.

Use password reset if you forget your password or need to change it securely.

# 4. Company Profile

The Company Profile is the organization's source-of-truth record for reporting, SSP generation, onboarding, and readiness context.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Company Profile Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

The Company Profile collects organizational identity, government identifiers, contacts, scope, providers, readiness metrics, and data quality flags.

## Who Uses It

- OrgAdmins and OrgOwners maintain it.
- Contributors, Assessors, and Viewers may view it depending on role access.
- Reports use this information where available.

## When to Use It

Complete the Company Profile before generating reports or performing formal readiness reviews.

## Editing Company Information

### Steps

1. Open **Company Profile**.
2. Select **Edit** if your role allows editing.
3. Complete organization identity fields.
4. Complete government information such as CAGE, UEI, DUNS, and NAICS where applicable.
5. Complete compliance profile fields such as FCI/CUI handling and assessment level.
6. Complete scope and location information.
7. Enter external provider details.
8. Save the profile.

### Expected Outcome

The Company Profile is saved and becomes available to reports such as SSP and POA&M.

## Important Fields

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Organization Identity | Legal Name, DBA Name, Website, Primary Contact, Secondary Contact |
| Government Information | CAGE Code, UEI, DUNS, NAICS Codes |
| Compliance Profile | Handles FCI, Handles CUI, Assessment Level, MSP/MSSP Used |
| Scope Profile | Headquarters, Additional Locations |
| External Providers | MSP, MSSP, Cloud Provider, Email Provider, Backup Provider |
| Readiness Metrics | Completion %, Practices Assessed, Evidence Count, Open POA&M Count, SPRS Score |
| Data Quality Flags | Missing CAGE, Missing UEI, Missing Contacts, Missing Locations |

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Complete legal name, CAGE, UEI, primary contact, locations, and FCI/CUI handling before generating an SSP.

## Overview

### Purpose

Assessments help your team track whether each practice and objective is met, not met, partially complete, or not yet assessed.

### Who Uses It

- OrgAdmins coordinate assessment work.
- Contributors update assigned practices and evidence.
- Assessors review readiness and evidence.
- Viewers may read assessment information.

### When to Use It

Use assessments throughout readiness work, especially during weekly progress reviews and before generating reports.

## CMMC Level 1

Level 1 is available for sponsored and commercial Level 1 organizations. It includes Level 1 domains and practices.

## CMMC Level 2

Level 2 is available for organizations with Level 2 access. If your tier does not include Level 2, the Level 2 section may be locked or hidden.

> **WARNING**  
> Do not use Level 2 report outputs for a Level 1-only organization unless your tier and program explicitly allow it.

## Domains, Practices, and Objectives

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | A grouped CMMC topic area, such as Access Control. |
| Practice | A specific CMMC practice requirement. |
| Objective | A detailed assessment objective associated with a practice. |

## Assessment Statuses

| Status | Suggested meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Met | The organization believes the practice/objective is satisfied and supported by evidence. |
| Partially Met | Work is underway or partly supported, but not complete. |
| Not Met | The practice/objective is not currently satisfied. |
| Not Assessed | No determination has been made yet. |

> **BEST PRACTICE**  
> Avoid marking a practice as Met until evidence is uploaded or clearly referenced.

## Notes

Notes capture implementation details, decisions, assumptions, and assessment comments.

### Steps

1. Open a domain.
2. Select a practice.
3. Review the practice and objectives.
4. Enter implementation notes or assessment comments.
5. Save progress.

### Expected Outcome

Notes remain available for future review and may support report preparation.

## Evidence

Evidence can be associated with practices or objectives and reused from the Evidence Library where available.

### Steps

1. Open the relevant practice or objective.
2. Select the evidence upload or attach option if your role allows it.
3. Upload or attach relevant evidence.
4. Save progress.

## AI Assistance

Practice Copilot and evidence intelligence features may help summarize, validate, or explain readiness information.

> **WARNING**  
> AI output should be reviewed by a qualified person. Do not treat AI assistance as a compliance determination.

## Saving Progress

Always save changes after updating statuses, notes, assignments, responsibility, or evidence references.

### Expected Outcome

Saved progress updates dashboard metrics, domain readiness, and report source data.

# 6. Evidence Library

The Evidence Library helps your organization store, review, and reuse evidence documents.

------------------------------------------------

[SCREENSHOT:
Evidence Library Page
]

------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

Evidence supports assessment decisions and report preparation. CMMC Launch Hub centralizes evidence metadata and upload workflows.

## Who Uses It

- OrgAdmins, Contributors, and Assessors may upload or manage evidence where permitted.
- Viewers may see evidence records if read access is allowed.
- Sponsor observers do not receive raw evidence files or filenames in sponsor dashboards.

## Uploading Evidence

### Steps

1. Open **Evidence Library** or a practice/objective evidence area.
2. Select the upload or attach evidence control.
3. Choose a document from your computer.
4. Add or confirm metadata where prompted.
5. Save or complete the upload.

### Expected Outcome

The evidence record appears in the organization evidence workspace and can support assessment work.

> **TIP**  
> Upload evidence as soon as each practice is completed to simplify SSP and report generation later.

## Searching Evidence

Use search and filters, where available, to find existing evidence before uploading a duplicate.

## Evidence Reuse

Evidence may support more than one practice or objective.

### Best Practice

When reusing evidence, confirm that the same document genuinely supports each linked requirement.

## OCR

OCR helps extract readable text from uploaded documents when supported.

### When to Use It

Use OCR for scanned or image-based files where text extraction may improve review.

> **NOTE**  
> OCR results should be reviewed. Scanned files, handwritten content, or low-quality images may produce incomplete text.

## Evidence Validation

Evidence validation assists with determining whether evidence appears relevant and usable.

> **WARNING**  
> Evidence validation is assistance only. Final evidence suitability should be reviewed by your organization and qualified advisors.

## Organizing Documents

Recommended naming pattern:

```text
Domain_Practice_EvidenceType_Date_Owner
```

Example:

```text
AC_3.1.1_Access_Control_Policy_2026-06-25_IT
```

# 14. Best Practices

## Recommended Workflow

Use this workflow for a new organization:

1. Complete registration.
2. Wait for approval.
3. Log in.
4. Complete Company Profile.
5. Review the dashboard.
6. Begin CMMC assessment.
7. Upload evidence as work is completed.
8. Review POA&M gaps.
9. Generate Executive Readiness Report.
10. Generate SSP, SPRS, or other reports when the data is ready.
11. Submit feedback or support requests as needed.

## Weekly Cadence

| Day/meeting | Suggested activity |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly readiness review | Review dashboard, domain readiness, open POA&M, and evidence gaps. |
| Evidence review | Confirm evidence is current, relevant, and mapped to correct practices. |
| Leadership update | Generate or review Executive Readiness Report. |
| Vendor review | Update vendor engagement status. |
| Admin review | Review users, pending invitations, and activity. |

## Evidence Strategy

- Upload evidence early.
- Use consistent file naming.
- Avoid duplicate uploads.
- Link evidence to the right practice/objective.
- Review OCR output.
- Keep expired or outdated evidence updated.

## Preparing for Assessments

- Confirm Company Profile is complete.
- Resolve missing CAGE, UEI, contact, and location fields.
- Review all Not Assessed items.
- Confirm evidence exists for Met practices.
- Review open POA&M items.

## Preparing for SSP

- Complete Company Profile.
- Review scope and provider fields.
- Confirm FCI/CUI handling.
- Complete assessment notes.
- Check evidence counts and references.

## Preparing for POA&M

- Identify Not Met and partially complete items.
- Assign owners.
- Set due dates where supported.
- Review progress weekly.

# 16. Frequently Asked Questions

1. **Does CMMC Launch Hub certify my organization?**  
   No. It helps manage readiness work but does not certify compliance.

2. **Who should complete registration?**  
   Usually the first OrgAdmin or primary compliance lead.

3. **What is the difference between commercial and sponsored registration?**  
   Commercial organizations join directly. Sponsored organizations join through an active program.

4. **Why is my registration pending?**  
   Registrations require administrator approval before full access.

5. **Can I use the platform before approval?**  
   No. Full access requires approval and activation.

6. **What should I do first after logging in?**  
   Complete Company Profile, then review the dashboard and begin assessment work.

7. **Why do I not see Level 2?**  
   Your tier may not include Level 2 access.

8. **What is SPONSORED tier?**  
   It is a sponsored/program baseline tier generally aligned to Level 1 restrictions.

9. **What is COMM_L1?**  
   Commercial Level 1 access.

10. **What is COMM_L2?**  
   Commercial Level 2 access with broader assessment and reporting access.

11. **Can viewers upload evidence?**  
   No. Viewers are read-only.

12. **Who can invite users?**  
   OrgAdmins, OrgOwners, and SuperAdmin users where applicable.

13. **Can I remove a user myself?**  
   User controls depend on your role. OrgAdmins should use the user management area.

14. **What is the Company Profile used for?**  
   It feeds readiness context, SSP, reports, and operational profile completeness.

15. **Do I need CAGE and UEI?**  
   Complete them if applicable. Missing fields may reduce profile completeness.

16. **What is evidence?**  
   Evidence is a document or record supporting a CMMC practice or objective.

17. **Can one evidence file support multiple practices?**  
   Yes, if it genuinely supports each linked requirement.

18. **Does OCR replace manual review?**  
   No. OCR is assistance only.

19. **Can sponsors see my files?**  
   No. Sponsor/program observers receive sanitized summaries and counts, not raw files.

20. **Can sponsors see my report contents?**  
   No. They see report flags/counts in sponsor-safe views.

21. **What is the Executive Readiness Report for?**  
   Leadership status and readiness review.

22. **What is an SSP?**  
   A System Security Plan used to organize system and security control context.

23. **Why do I not see SSP export?**  
   Your tier or role may not allow it.

24. **What is SPRS?**  
   Supplier Performance Risk System scoring support.

25. **What is POA&M?**  
   Plan of Action and Milestones, used to track gaps and remediation.

26. **What is the Marketplace?**  
   A vendor directory for CMMC-related providers and resources.

27. **Are marketplace vendors endorsed?**  
   Marketplace listing is informational. Evaluate vendors independently.

28. **Can I review a vendor?**  
   Authorized organization users may submit reviews, which require moderation.

29. **Why is my review not visible?**  
   Reviews must be approved before appearing publicly.

30. **What is Vendor Engagement?**  
   A record showing whether your organization is evaluating, actively using, or has completed work with a vendor.

31. **Can vendor engagements store contracts?**  
   No. The baseline tracks metadata, not contracts, pricing, private messages, or raw files.

32. **What is a Sponsor Observer?**  
   A read-only user who can view authorized pilot or program oversight summaries.

33. **What is a Program Observer?**  
   A sponsor observer scoped to one or more assigned programs.

34. **Can Program Observers see all organizations?**  
   No. Program Observers are scoped to assigned programs.

35. **Can Program Observers approve registrations?**  
   No. They are read-only.

36. **What is Recent Activity?**  
   A metadata history of important actions.

37. **Can I edit an activity record?**  
   No. Activity is intended as audit/operational metadata.

38. **Are AI recommendations available for vendors?**  
   Future Enhancement: readiness-aware vendor recommendations are not implemented in v1.1.

39. **What if a report seems wrong?**  
   Review Company Profile, assessment data, evidence, and POA&M items, then regenerate.

40. **Where do I get support?**  
   Use the Support page or Send Feedback button inside CMMC Launch Hub.
