# CMMC Launch Hub Pilot Enrollment Guide

Date: 2026-06-05  
Audience: Pilot organizations, sponsors, and onboarding support

## Enrollment Overview

The pilot enrolls a small cohort of 3-5 Connecticut manufacturers and defense contractors. Each organization receives guided onboarding, a scoped pilot workspace, and direct founder support.

## Enrollment Fit

Good pilot candidates:

- Are Connecticut-based or Connecticut-connected manufacturers, suppliers, or defense contractors.
- Need CMMC Level 1 or Level 2 readiness organization.
- Have an internal lead who can act as OrgAdmin.
- Can identify pilot users and roles before onboarding.
- Are willing to provide structured feedback.

## Information Needed Before Setup

Collect the following before creating or approving the organization:

- Legal organization name.
- DBA name if applicable.
- Primary contact name, email, and phone.
- Secondary contact if available.
- Website.
- CAGE Code if available.
- UEI if available.
- Optional DUNS.
- NAICS codes.
- Expected CMMC level.
- Whether the organization handles FCI or CUI.
- Approximate employee, user, and location counts.
- MSP/MSSP and external providers if known.
- Initial pilot user list and intended roles.

## Pilot Roles

| Role | Typical Use |
| ---- | ---- |
| OrgAdmin / OrgOwner | Owns profile, users, invitations, assessment coordination, evidence, and reporting. |
| Assessor | Reviews practices/objectives and contributes to assessment workflow. |
| Contributor | Adds assessment input and evidence where permitted. |
| Viewer | Reviews permitted information without mutation controls. |

## Enrollment Workflow

1. Confirm the organization fits the pilot cohort.
2. Confirm the primary OrgAdmin.
3. Approve or create the organization in the SuperAdmin workflow.
4. Confirm organization tier and subscription status.
5. Confirm initial users and roles.
6. Send invitations or support invited-user login creation as appropriate.
7. Schedule guided onboarding.
8. Confirm users can sign in and see the correct organization.
9. Complete the Company Profile source-of-truth fields.
10. Open Command Dashboard and review the organization snapshot.

## First Onboarding Session

During the first guided session:

- Confirm organization identity and tier.
- Complete Company Profile fields.
- Review role-specific access.
- Walk through Command Dashboard.
- Open one assessment practice and objective.
- Demonstrate evidence upload and OCR expectations.
- Generate or preview Executive Readiness and POA&M reporting paths.
- Explain support intake and feedback process.

## Security Checks During Enrollment

- Confirm OrgAdmin does not see SuperAdmin controls.
- Confirm users only see their assigned organization.
- Confirm Viewer users do not see evidence upload or archive controls.
- Confirm Contributor, Assessor, and Viewer do not see organization user-management controls.
- Escalate immediately if cross-organization data appears.

## Enrollment Completion

Enrollment is complete when:

- The organization is active.
- The OrgAdmin can sign in.
- Initial users have expected access.
- Company Profile is reviewed.
- Command Dashboard loads.
- Assessment, evidence, and reporting workflows have been introduced.
- Support and feedback expectations are understood.
